Planning panel and Home task cards

Wireframes for the release built from ADR 0.58.0 (docs/adrs in the Greymere repo). All four were approved by the author on 3 Nov 2026. Sample content is invented; the fields are the real ones from /api/home/tasks, /api/schedule/daily-list and /api/planning/board. Tap on a card opens its detail; swipe left defers; swipe right marks done.

Columns are phases, three at a time, swiping for the rest. The rail shows all nine with a dot where a book sits. Card: cover, title, current step, progress against expected (the tick), baseline window and publish date, drift chip, percent. Press and hold lifts a card; dropping on a phase opens a step picker; a move that skips steps or a gate asks first and logs the override as the desktop does. Closed books are hidden.
