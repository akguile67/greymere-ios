Greymere iOS 0.4.0 — Dictation wireframes

Drawn against ADR 0.53.0 and dictation-exit-checklist.md, captured 29 October 2026. Boxes are placeholders, not final styling. Dictation lives in the one place the app has a text field, the Godric chat input; the mic slot reserved in 0.3.0 becomes the button. One layout for all sizes, as before. The app shows what the server returns and changes nothing itself: no rewriting, no vocabulary, no AI call on the device. Nothing is built until Sprint 1's go/no-go says go.

Typed or fixed text: ordinary pending text: can still revise (dotted, lighter) Substituted: the server replaced a heard word (solid amber underline)

1 · The button, in its three states

To decide at this wireframe: the ADR says "a thumb button within reach of the thumb". Shown here as the existing mic slot in the input row, which already sits at the bottom edge in thumb reach, enlarged a little and red while listening. The alternative is a separate larger floating button above the input. This is the cheaper and quieter option; say if you want the floating one.

2 · Live text in the field

Words appear as you speak, about a second behind. Pending text can still change; at each pause it is fixed and stops changing. You can type, move the cursor or fix a word at any time. Nothing is sent to Godric until you press Send.

Tap an amber word: what you said, and one action, Undo. Undo restores the heard text and tells the server, which learns not to do it again.

3 · Fixing a word teaches Greymere

There is no separate step. If you change a dictated word and move on, the app quietly reports the fix and the server may learn it (it needs a second identical fix for ordinary English words). The only sign on the phone is a brief note. The list of what has been learned is on the desktop; there is no corrections screen in the app in 0.4.0.

Not drawn, on purpose

A corrections review screen on iOS (desktop only in this release); a settings screen for dictation (no settings: the model, prompt and rules are the server's); hold-to-talk; dictation in any field other than the chat input; any display of cost or of the Whisper model in use.
