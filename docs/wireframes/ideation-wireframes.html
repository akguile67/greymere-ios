Greymere iOS 0.5.0 — Ideation and panel menu wireframes

Drawn against the desktop Ideation panel as captured on 1 November 2026 (see ideation-exit-checklist.md). Revised after review: workflow-only tray, settings gear at top right, no environment band, Godric in the pill. Boxes are placeholders, not final styling. Client-only: no server change.

1 · The glass bar and the workflow tray

The bottom of every screen carries the floating glass bar: Home on the left, the Ask Godric pill in the centre, Panels on the right. Panels (or pulling the screen up, as Apple Music does) raises a glass tray holding the workflow stages only, in the desktop rail's order. Back office is left out. The purple environment band is gone, which gives the height back.

2 · Settings at the top right

A gear (or a small head, your call) at the top right of every screen drops a menu. For now it holds one thing: About, showing the app and server versions. What else goes in Settings waits for the Configurator panel. A pre-production or dev build shows a small tag beside the title (the production app shows nothing), so a test build can never be mistaken for the real one.

3 · Ideation: synopsis first, Godric on call

Choosing Ideation in the tray opens the book's current synopsis. The book chip drops down a list to change book. Ask Godric raises the Ideation conversation over the synopsis ("I'm not happy with this synopsis, let's look at the changes"). As you talk Godric edits the synopsis behind the sheet; Close brings the synopsis back with a mark showing it changed. The same sheet is the Godric of every panel; only the thread changes.
