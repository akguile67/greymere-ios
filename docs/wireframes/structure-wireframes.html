Structure panel — wireframes for iOS 0.10.0

Sprint 2, step 1 of ADR 0.60.0 (Addendum 1) and the signed-off parity checklist (4 Nov 2026). Targets: iPad mini 7 (landscape 1133×744 pt, portrait 744×1133) and iPhone 14 Plus (428×926). Sample content is invented. Shape of Structure: the Ideation synopsis names the Prologue, acts and Epilogue; those become the acts, whose text is a reminder of intent only; the author adds the chapters to each act, briefs each on six fields, and drags cards to reorder within the act; Creation (built) is where beats and prose are written. Not drawn, by decision: editing, locking or unlocking an act synopsis; Validate structure; Godric History and New chat; Generate synopsis from manuscript; Generate briefs from prose. The glass bar and the Settings gear are as in the app shell. Approval of these gates the navigation prototype.

Tap a chapter card to open it as its own screen (phone push). Opening an empty card makes it Active and it becomes Godric's focus hint.

The summary sits at the top; the six fields are sections that expand and collapse, as on the desktop (here Summary headline and What happens are open). While not Briefed each is editable and saved on leaving it, without committing. Commit sends what is on screen and refuses an all-empty brief.

Briefed is read-only; Unlock briefing to edit drops it to Active. Draft › (Briefed only) opens Creation on that chapter. The ⋯ menu holds Rename and Delete. The triangle names missing codex entries and stops showing once the entry exists. Committing in a drafted act reopens it, and the act badge follows.

Live refresh follows structure.changed: the board and open card update in place and a banner names what changed. A field you are typing in is never overwritten; a clash goes to the sheet.

Stale write: rename, brief save, commit and reorder send the version they were based on; on 409 the server returns the current card, used to list the fields that differ. One choice for the whole card: Use this device re-sends against the current version; Use the other device drops your edit. Same pattern as the Codex "Apply refused".

11. Layout questions: decided

All decided by the author.

  1. iPad portrait: phone-style drill-down, as Codex. Landscape keeps the split.
  2. Board: one act open at a time.
  3. Act synopsis: opens closed, with an expand control; read-only.
  4. Chapter on phone: a pushed screen, summary on top, the six fields as expand/collapse sections.
  5. Reorder on phone: hold and drag within the act, as on iPad. No Move up/down, no Reorder mode.
  6. Add chapter: ask for a title first; all details editable afterwards.
  7. Numbering: sequential on the server (0.60.0); later chapters renumber, no warning.
  8. Staged brief: banner plus Commit, no Discard; staged fields are editable before Commit.
  9. Stale sheet: one choice for the whole card, listing the differing fields.
  10. Godric: docked third column on iPad, sheet on iPhone.

Still to confirm: the summary shown at the top of the card is the Summary headline field, which is also listed as the first section (drawn expanded). Say if the top summary should be something else.
